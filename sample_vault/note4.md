# Quantum Mechanics Basics

Quantum mechanics describes how matter and energy behave at very small scales, where particles stop acting like tiny balls and start acting like spread-out waves of probability.

## Wave Function

A particle's state is described by a wave function, a mathematical object that gives the probability of finding the particle in a particular position or with a particular momentum when it is measured. Before measurement, the particle does not have a single definite location.

## Uncertainty Principle

Heisenberg's uncertainty principle states that you cannot simultaneously know a particle's exact position and exact momentum. The more precisely one is known, the less precisely the other can be known. This is not a limitation of our instruments, it is a fundamental property of nature.
